The Story So Far

Hi, We're Diamond Dive Treasures.

A small, ocean-obsessed jewelry shop based in Titusville, Florida.

Our Mission

Everyday Treasures, No Deep-Sea Diving Required

Diamond Dive Treasures LLC was founded right here in Titusville, Florida — home to rocket launches, river tides, and some of the best sunrise beach walks around. We wanted to bottle up that feeling of finding a little treasure in the sand and turn it into jewelry you can wear every day.

We're a small, family-run business based in Titusville, FL, and every piece we choose to carry is picked with the same question in mind: "Would I be excited to find this washed up on the beach?" If the answer's yes, it earns a spot in the treasure chest.

What We Believe

Our Values

  • Fun over fussy. Jewelry should make you smile, not require a safety deposit box. Expect playful designs at prices that feel good.
  • Comfortable to wear. We favor sterling silver, stainless steel, and other nickel-free, tarnish-resistant materials — no real diamonds or fine gemstones, just as much sparkle.
  • Small and intentional. As a small LLC just starting out, we curate a tight, thoughtful collection rather than an overwhelming catalog.
  • Honest & local. We're a real small business based in Titusville, FL, and we stand behind everything we sell — see our Returns & Refunds page for the details.

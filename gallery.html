A Note on Our Materials

Every piece is made with sterling silver, stainless steel, freshwater pearls, sea glass, or cubic zirconia (CZ) simulated stones. We do not sell real diamonds or fine loose gemstones — just fun, wearable sparkle at a price that matches. Have sensitive ears or skin? Most of our pieces are nickel-free — check each listing or reach out on our Contact page.
